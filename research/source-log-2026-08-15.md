## Editorial rule
Sources are discovery and verification signals, not copy sources. WorthGo stores its own concise descriptions, practical trade-offs and recommendation metadata. Ratings are kept as research signals and never presented as WorthGo-owned ratings.

## Verification policy
A candidate should be promoted only when the place identity, city/area and broad category are sufficiently corroborated. Hours, menus and pricing are treated as changeable and should be rechecked before any future automated or transactional feature.
