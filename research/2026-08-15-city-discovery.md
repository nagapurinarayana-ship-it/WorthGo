# WorthGo Research — Hyderabad + Dubai

Research pass: 15 Aug 2026

## Editorial rule

WorthGo combines multiple independent signals before publishing a recommendation. Search results, business listings/review signals, official tourism sources, local publications/blogs and community discussions are discovery inputs—not copy sources. We write original `whyGo` and `whySkip` guidance and do not copy reviews or ratings.

A place should not enter the public dataset merely because one blog lists it. Prefer at least two independent signals for businesses, and an official source for factual attraction details where available.

### Community signal
Recent r/hyderabad discussions were used to discover recurring local recommendations and counterexamples, including breakfast/tiffin, old food institutions, non-obvious experiences and affordable food. Community opinions are treated as subjective signals, not facts.

### Business/review signal
Current business search results were used to verify that candidate businesses exist and to capture current review-count/rating/hours signals where available. WorthGo should store these as research metadata rather than present them as a proprietary WorthGo score.

## Important editorial safeguards

1. Do not copy review text.
2. Do not claim a place is a "hidden gem" solely because one article calls it that.
3. Do not publish exact prices/hours unless recently verified for the specific branch.
4. Separate business-level facts from editorial judgments.
5. Re-check closed/relocated/renamed places before publication.
6. Prefer branch-specific records where a brand has multiple locations.
7. Keep `lastVerified` on every published record.
8. Use review volume as a confidence signal, not as the recommendation itself.
9. Keep negative signals (`whySkip`) because a recommendation should be honest, not promotional.
10. Never pay to rank a place in the recommendation engine.
