# WorthGo Research & Quality Rules

Updated: 2026-08-15

## Source hierarchy

1. Official tourism / government / attraction / business sources — factual authority.
2. Current Google Business information and review signals — existence, location, current operating signals, recurring review themes.
3. Established local publications and guides — discovery and local context.
4. Tripadvisor / Zomato / District / other major discovery platforms — secondary cross-check.
5. Reddit and local community discussions — discovery signal only; never sufficient by itself.

## Publication rule

A place should not enter the primary recommendation pool solely because one blog or one social post calls it “best”. Prefer at least two independent signals, or one authoritative source for an attraction plus a current existence/location check.

## Confidence score

WorthGo computes a 0–100 research confidence score separately from user-fit ranking.

- Source quality: 0–25
- Cross-source agreement: 0–20
- Review depth: 0–15
- Freshness: 0–15
- Editorial completeness: 0–15
- Operational certainty: 0–10

Confidence bands:

- 85–100: Verified — eligible for Best Match
- 70–84: Strong — eligible for normal recommendations
- 55–69: Researching — eligible only when no stronger match exists
- below 55: Hold — do not recommend prominently

## Review signals

Review ratings/counts are research signals, not WorthGo ratings. Do not copy review text. Use recurring themes only when they are supported by multiple observations.

High review volume increases confidence but never overrides a serious recent negative signal.

## Hidden-gem rule

A place can be labelled `hiddenGem` only when it has meaningful local/discovery evidence and is not simply an obvious tourist landmark. A high review count does not automatically make a place famous or not-hidden; editorial context matters.

## Safety / stale data

Do not publish exact opening hours, prices, availability, closures, or safety claims unless recently verified. If uncertain, omit the fact or mark it as needs-review.

Do not recommend isolated or potentially unsafe locations merely because community posts call them hidden gems.

## WorthGo editorial rule

Every published place should answer:

- Why go?
- Why skip?
- Who is it for?
- How much time does it take?
- What budget range should the user expect?
- Why did WorthGo select it?

The product's value is the synthesis of many sources into a useful decision, not reproducing another site's ranking.
