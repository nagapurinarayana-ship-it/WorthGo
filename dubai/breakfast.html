Breakfast in Dubai,
without overthinking it.

A useful Dubai breakfast is not always the most expensive brunch. Start with the neighbourhood, budget and pace you want, then choose a place that fits the rest of your morning.

Al Ustad Special Kebab — Bur Dubai

WorthGo includes this long-running Iranian institution for its strong local character, value and memorable atmosphere. It is better suited to a casual food-focused outing than a quiet café morning.

Typical budget signal: AED 25–100. Good for: curious visitors and locals who value character.

Ravi Restaurant — Al Satwa

A decades-old Pakistani institution and useful value option when you want a straightforward local meal rather than a polished brunch experience.

Typical budget signal: AED 20–60. Good for: budget-focused, casual eating.
