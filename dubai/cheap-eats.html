DUBAI · BUDGET FOOD

Eat well in Dubai
without the splurge.

Dubai's best-value food is concentrated in older neighbourhoods such as Satwa, Karama, Deira and Bur Dubai. These are the places where a quick meal can be genuinely local rather than a compromise.

Al Mallah
Satwa · Lebanese street food · about AED 10–50

Shawarma, fresh juices and a classic casual setting.

Ravi Restaurant
Al Satwa · Pakistani · about AED 25–75

A long-running value institution for karahi, biryani and late-night meals.

Al Ustad Special Kabab
Bur Dubai · Iranian · about AED 25–80

Old-school kebabs and a memorable character-filled room.

Calicut Paragon
Karama · Kerala · about AED 50–150

Kerala biryani, seafood and family-style meals.

Raju Omlet
Karama · eggs · low-cost

Simple egg dishes and breakfast-style comfort food.

Ashwaq Cafeteria
Al Fahidi · shawarma · low-cost

An old-school cheap-eat option for a quick shawarma.

Karachi Darbar Deira
Deira · Pakistani-Indian · budget

A practical choice when you want generous South Asian food without a premium setting.

Chaat Bazaar
Dubai · Indian street food · budget

A snack-first option for chaat and quick bites.
