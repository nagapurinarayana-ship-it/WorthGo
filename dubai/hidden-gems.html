How WorthGo defines a Dubai hidden gem

A hidden gem does not have to be unknown. We use the label for places that are easy to miss in a typical tourist itinerary but can be a strong fit because of neighbourhood character, cuisine, value or a distinctive experience. Current hours, menus and availability should still be verified before a visit.
