HYDERABAD · BREAKFAST

Breakfast in Hyderabad,
done right.

The best breakfast choice depends on the morning. For speed and value, look at tiffin centers. For chai and heritage, look at Irani cafes. For a slower weekend, choose a café or brunch room.

Ram Ki Bandi

Very early dosa/idli stop near the Old City. Best for a fast, casual local breakfast.

Babai Hotel

Idli and tiffin-style breakfast with a strong local signal.

Nimrah Cafe & Bakery

Irani chai, bakery staples and an Old City setting.

Cafe Niloufer

Classic chai-and-bakery experience for a slower start.

Panchakattu Dosa

Dosa-focused breakfast in Jubilee Hills.

Taaza Kitchen

Madhapur option for quick South Indian breakfast.

The Roastery Coffee House

Coffee-led brunch when you want more time and a café setting.

Varalakshmi Tiffins

Gachibowli breakfast for pesarattu, upma and idli.

Hyderabad breakfast questions

What type of breakfast is Hyderabad known for?

Popular choices include South Indian tiffin such as idli and dosa, Irani chai with bakery items, and modern café or brunch options.

Where should I start for a quick Hyderabad breakfast?

Start with a tiffin centre near your area and verify its current opening hours before travelling.

Are restaurant hours and prices guaranteed?

No. Hours, menus and prices can change, so confirm time-sensitive details with the venue before visiting.
