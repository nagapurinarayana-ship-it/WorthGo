HYDERABAD · BIRYANI

Best biryani in Hyderabad:
5 classic stops.

Hyderabad has too many biryani places for one universal winner. WorthGo's first shortlist focuses on five established names that continue to appear in current Hyderabad biryani discovery results, then explains which area and type of visit each one suits.

Research checked August 31, 2026. Branches, menus, prices and hours can change; verify the exact location before travelling.

OLD CITY

Hotel Shadab

📍 Ghansi Bazaar / Old City

A practical pick when biryani is part of an Old City outing. Choose it when you are already planning Charminar-side sightseeing and want a classic Hyderabadi meal rather than crossing the city for a standalone stop.

CENTRAL HYDERABAD

Cafe Bahar

📍 Basheer Bagh

Useful for a central-city biryani plan near Himayatnagar, Basheer Bagh and nearby business areas. It is a stronger fit when convenience and an established local name matter more than a polished destination-dining experience.

RTC X ROADS

Bawarchi

📍 RTC X Roads

A long-running name that makes sense when your route already passes through RTC X Roads, Himayatnagar or the central-north corridor. Good for a direct food-first stop rather than a long café-style meal.

SECUNDERABAD

Paradise Biryani

📍 S D Road, Secunderabad

The original-brand association makes the S D Road area the useful reference point for visitors who specifically want to try Paradise. Because the brand has many branches, verify which outlet you intend to visit instead of assuming every branch offers the same experience.

OLD CITY / WEST

Shah Ghouse

📍 Charminar-area listings and multiple Hyderabad branches

A strong candidate when you want a busy, no-frills Hyderabadi meal and have a convenient Shah Ghouse branch on your route. Branch selection matters, so check the exact address before setting out.

How WorthGo built this shortlist

The page prioritizes established places that remain visible in current Hyderabad biryani discovery sources, then adds practical area context. On August 31, Zomato's Hyderabad biryani collection listed Shadab, Cafe Bahar, Paradise and Bawarchi among its city picks, while current Swiggy results also showed Shah Ghouse as an active biryani option. This is not a claim that five restaurants are objectively better than every other kitchen in Hyderabad.

Taste varies by batch, branch, dish and preference. WorthGo therefore treats “best” as a decision guide: established reputation + current discoverability + a reason the stop makes sense for a real Hyderabad route.
